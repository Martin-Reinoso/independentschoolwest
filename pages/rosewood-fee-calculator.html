How many children will attend?

Before you rely on this estimate

What is included?

✓

Resource levy included

The $910 Student Resource Levy is included for every child and is not reduced by any discount.

✓

Discount order applied

The $1,000 Foundation Year discount is deducted first. Any applicable sibling, bond and annual-payment discounts are then calculated on the remaining tuition.

!

Uniforms not included

Uniform costs and any items not priced in the 2027 fee schedule are outside this estimate.

Calculation assumptions and limitations

The $1,000 Foundation Year discount is deducted from tuition first. Any applicable sibling, bond and annual-payment discounts are then calculated on the remaining tuition.

Each percentage stage is rounded to the nearest cent. Term amounts divide estimated annual tuition across four school terms, with any rounding cents placed in the earliest terms. Part-year enrolment, withdrawal, bond upgrades and fee adjustments are not modelled.

Draft 01

Enrolment Policy

Purpose. Rosewood intends to provide a clear, fair and family-centred process for considering applications while planning for student wellbeing, learning and safety.

Principles

  • The dignity and best interests of each child.
  • Partnership with parents as the primary educators of their children.
  • Transparent criteria, reasonable adjustments and non-discriminatory consideration.
  • Collection of only information reasonably required for enrolment and planning.
  • Child safety, privacy and procedural fairness.

Stages and decisions

An expression of interest is not an application. An invitation is not an offer. An application is considered only after required information, evidence and signatures are received. Interviews do not guarantee an offer. Enrolment becomes final only through an authorised written offer and completed enrolment agreement.

The final policy must approve admissions priorities, capacity rules, review, waitlist, withdrawal, deferral, correction and false-information procedures.

Draft 02

Enrolment Procedure

  1. Rosewood records an expression of interest.
  2. Authorised staff may issue a time-limited private invitation.
  3. The invited guardian verifies the nominated mailbox with an OTP.
  4. The family completes the application and required evidence.
  5. Each required guardian reviews the same frozen revision and signs independently.
  6. Rosewood confirms receipt after the required signature set is complete.
  7. Authorised staff review, clarify and arrange any planning conversation.
  8. The family receives a written outcome and, where applicable, a separate offer and enrolment agreement.

Staff controls

  • Never request sensitive documents through ordinary email.
  • Keep incomplete drafts separate from submitted applications.
  • Record material corrections and authorised overrides.
  • Do not infer legal authority from a contact preference.
  • Use organisation-controlled accounts and role-based access.

Draft 03

Privacy Collection Notice - Enrolment

Rosewood College, being established by Families for Education, proposes to collect information to consider enrolment, communicate with families, plan educational and wellbeing support, meet safety and legal responsibilities, administer offers and maintain accountable records.

Information may include student and guardian identity/contact details, primary and shared-care household addresses, independent emergency contact details, family and care arrangements, education history, health/support information, permissions, residency information, documents, declarations, signatures and security/audit metadata.

Information may be disclosed where necessary to authorised Rosewood personnel, service providers operating enrolment systems, professional advisers, previous education providers with consent, relevant health professionals when the conditional consent is given, and regulators or authorities where authorised or required. Final disclosures, the exact scope of health-provider contact and overseas-processing details are not approved in this placeholder.

The V2 test architecture uses AWS services in the Sydney region and restricted Google Drive/Sheets. If required information is not provided, Rosewood may be unable to assess or safely plan an application. Optional fields and optional community communication are identified.

The final notice must identify the legal entity, privacy contact, access/correction route, complaint process, retention, approved disclosures and service-provider locations.

Draft 04

Privacy Policy

Rosewood intends to handle personal and sensitive information lawfully, fairly, transparently and only for defined educational, safety, pastoral and administrative purposes.

  • Collect only information with an identified purpose.
  • Protect information in transit and at rest.
  • Restrict access according to role and need.
  • Maintain correction, access, withdrawal and complaint pathways.
  • Maintain retention, secure deletion and incident-response rules.
  • Avoid sensitive values in logs, URLs, analytics and ordinary email.

Draft 05

Parent, Guardian And Carer Partnership Code

Rosewood recognises parents as the primary educators of their children and seeks a respectful partnership between home and school.

  • Place student safety, dignity and learning first.
  • Communicate honestly, respectfully and through appropriate channels.
  • Protect the privacy and reputation of students, families and staff.
  • Raise concerns early and engage constructively in resolution.
  • Respect the Catholic identity and educational mission of the school.
  • Follow child-safety directions and keep important family information current.

Draft 10

Admission And Signing Guidance

Legal-review warning. This working note preserves scenarios the workflow must handle. It is not legal advice and does not adopt another school's signing rules as Rosewood policy.

Scenarios requiring an approved rule

  • A student over 15 who is living independently.
  • A parent as understood under the Family Law Act 1975 (Cth).
  • Separated parents and whether both signatures are required.
  • A current court or parenting order affecting contact, responsibility, information sharing or school decision-making.
  • An informal, kinship or other carer who has day-to-day care and with whom the student regularly lives.
  • A secondary student completing relevant sections and co-signing.

Source guidance retained for review, not adopted

The mapped source process stated that, absent a current court order, parents of a child under 18 may have parental responsibility; it requested both parents' signatures for separated parents or a relevant order. For an informal carer, it contemplated a statutory declaration, day-to-day care, ordinary consent such as excursions, a 12-month declaration period and a legally responsible parent's wishes prevailing in a dispute. Rosewood must confirm the current legal position, exceptions, evidence, duration, dispute and child-safety rules before using any of that model.

Workflow controls

  • Keep legal responsibility, contact permission and required-signatory status separate.
  • Do not infer authority from the primary contact, home address, fee payer or expression-of-interest submitter.
  • Escalate uncertainty, disputes and restricted contact to authorised staff.
  • Require approved signers to verify and sign the same frozen revision independently.

Included for every student

$910 Student Resource Levy

The annual levy helps cover the school experiences and learning resources families often have to budget for separately.

  • Books and stationery
  • IT equipment
  • Camps and excursions
  • Incursions
  • Specialised learning resources
  • Student printed material

The levy is included in calculator totals and is not reduced by tuition discounts.

Choose your payment rhythm

Term or annual payment

Four invoices

Pay by term

Tuition is invoiced at the start of each term. The full Student Resource Levy is included in the Term 1 invoice. Each invoice is due within 14 days.

Additional 5% tuition reduction

Pay for the year

Pay the full annual school fee at the start of Term 1. The 5% reduction applies after the Foundation, sibling and bond reductions. Payment is due within 14 days.

Refundable family bond

Choose the balance that works for your family.

Every family selects a bond separate from annual school fees. The bond helps fund school buildings and long-term financial stability, replacing an annual development levy.

Lower bond

Option A

One bond per family, paid when the first child enrols. No additional bond is required when siblings join.

5% off first child's tuition

Option B

$10,000

Paid in the first year. The first child receives a 5% tuition reduction every year at Rosewood, up to Year 12.

↩Returned when the first child leaves Rosewood.

5% off every child's tuition

Option B for siblings

$20,000

Families with two or more children may add a second $10,000 bond. This is the maximum bond, regardless of family size.

↩The first $10,000 is returned when the first child leaves; the second $10,000 when the last child leaves.

Important to know

What sits outside these figures?

  • Uniforms are paid separately by families.
  • The calculator assumes a full 2027 calendar year and, for Option B comparisons, the selected bond paid at the start.
  • All percentage stages are rounded to the nearest cent.
  • Opening in 2027 remains subject to approval by the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority.

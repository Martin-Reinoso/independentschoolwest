Children attending Rosewood at the same time

From the fifth child onwards, tuition is fully reduced. Only the $910 Student Resource Levy applies to each additional child.

For more than 20 children attending together, ask the College for a tailored estimate.

+

Sibling tuition reductions are applied automatically and increase for additional children.The second child receives 15% off tuition, the third 30%, the fourth 45%, and the fifth and every subsequent child 100%. The resource levy still applies.

Term payments divide tuition across four invoices. The full Student Resource Levy is included in Term 1.

How discounts compound: the $1,000 Foundation Year reduction is applied first. Sibling, bond and annual-payment percentage reductions then apply one after another to the tuition remaining, so percentage discounts multiply rather than add.

Option B timing: the 5% tuition reduction applies immediately. The estimate covers a full calendar year and assumes the selected bond is paid at the start.

Bond refunds: Option A is returned when the last family member leaves. Under Option B, the first $10,000 is returned when the first child leaves and the second $10,000 is returned when the last child leaves.

Not included: uniforms and other family-paid items.

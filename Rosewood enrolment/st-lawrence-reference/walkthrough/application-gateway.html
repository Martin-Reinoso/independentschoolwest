Dear Parent/Guardian,

Provide the details requested below to begin applying for your child's admission to the school.

If your family has contacted or visited the school before, existing contact information may be used to prepopulate the application.

Use the same email address supplied during the earlier enquiry.

Before beginning, review the Enrolment Policy and the Enrolment Procedure for MACS schools.

Prepare the following documents for the upload stage at the end of the online application:

  • Birth certificate
  • Immunisation statement
  • Sacramental certificates, such as a baptismal certificate
  • Temporary or permanent residency-status document, if applicable
  • Most recent school report and NAPLAN report
  • Court order or restriction documentation, if applicable
  • Specialist reports, if applicable

For information about the handling of personal information, read the Privacy Policy and Privacy Collection Notice - Parents and Students.

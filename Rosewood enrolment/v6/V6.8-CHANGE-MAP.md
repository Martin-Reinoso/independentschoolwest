# V6.8 Application Change Map

## Release Identity

- EOI form: `rosewood-eoi-2026.8` (questions unchanged; shared assets repinned)
- Application form: `rosewood-application-2026.8`
- Schema: `rosewood-v6-2026-08-08-form-v8`
- Earlier definitions remain immutable and addressable.

## Family Selector And Save-Later

- Direct applications show no invitation-source label.
- An explicitly EOI-linked child may show: “Information from your Expression of
  Interest has been included.”
- Staff operational source tracking remains unchanged.
- Save and continue later flushes the draft and active uploads, revokes only the
  selected child editing/status sessions, clears that child's answers from page memory
  and retains the verified family session.
- The confirmation offers Return to child applications. Sign out remains the only
  family-session exit from the selector.

## Student Interface

- The previous-attendance question and separate Previous Education section are retired.
- Interrupted schooling remains required and now follows the current-school question.
- The separate Student Residence section is retired. Address sharing and a compact
  single-select Home Care Arrangement are part of Student Primary Address.
- Other care and Shared Custody retain their conditional required details.
- Student and guardian address controls use `autocomplete="off"`; no external address
  lookup or tracking service is called.
- The three student country controls use aligned desktop labels and return to natural
  height on mobile.
- “Victorian law” links to the official Health Victoria primary-school immunisation
  requirements page. The new tab is labelled for assistive technology.

## Optional Survey

The following optional fields appear after the three required Conditions agreements:

1. Special aptitude subjects
2. Preferred school subjects
3. Subjects where special help may be needed
4. Main hobbies or cultural pursuits
5. Sport participation
6. Extra-curricular activities
7. Local-library membership
8. What attracts the family to the school
9. Personal qualities the family would like developed
10. Value of the mentoring system to parents
11. Intended years of study

Answers are accepted by the versioned contract, retained in immutable draft/submission
revisions, included in the frozen guardian review and appended to the Google Sheets
Conditions projection. Blank survey answers do not block navigation or submission.

## Compatibility And Recovery

- Submitted V6.7 and earlier applications never upgrade.
- An editable older draft upgrades through the existing conditional version-upgrade
  path. Existing answer keys are preserved.
- Retired previous-education values may remain in an upgraded authoritative answer map
  and history. They are not displayed in the V6.8 applicant or guardian review and are
  not erased.
- Existing Sheets columns retain their positions. Eleven survey columns append after
  `parent_carer_agreement_acknowledged`.
- A stale browser contract is rejected with `FORM_VERSION_MISMATCH` rather than writing
  under the wrong definition.

## Verification Contract

Automated coverage must verify the selector copy, save-later family return, retired UI,
conditional care controls, autocomplete settings, country alignment, official link,
all eleven survey fields, optional validation, split/projection persistence, frozen
review, immutable V6.7 definition and append-only Sheets headers. Desktop and mobile
browser checks must confirm no horizontal overflow and usable long labels.

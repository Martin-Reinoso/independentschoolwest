Release date: 1 September 2026

- Current EOI: `rosewood-eoi-2026.24`
- Current Application: `rosewood-application-2026.25`

- Earlier EOI/Application definitions remain addressable and supported. Active drafts retain their answers and continue through the versioned compatibility path.

## Staff workspaces

- **Application Review** shows the authoritative application, protected document previews, signature evidence, revision history and staff-only review controls. It has no family-email, communication-history or booking-invitation actions.
- **Family communications** owns application selection, permitted recipients, message history, draft, staff test and explicit reviewed send.
- **Principal meetings** owns schedule creation, reviewed batch time creation and private family invitations.

## Family booking

1. Staff creates a meeting schedule and one or more future available times.
2. Staff selects an application and a server-permitted recipient, then explicitly sends a private booking invitation.
3. The family opens the link, enters the invited email and verifies an OTP.
4. The family books one available slot.
5. The same family may later reopen the same link, verify the same email and replace its slot with another available slot.
6. The change keeps one booking/application identity and atomically releases/reserves slots. No family delete/cancel action exists.

## Integrity controls

- Server-side contact permission controls communication and meeting recipients.
- Viewer-role staff cannot open the communications or meeting-management workspaces.
- A conditional scope record blocks duplicate active invitations for the same application, schedule and recipient email.
- Conditional DynamoDB transactions prevent slot overwrite and partial booking changes.
- Application records and submitted revisions are outside communication and booking transactions.
- Case/meeting actions remain audited and operational email remains durable through the outbox.

Rosewood intends to handle personal and sensitive information lawfully, fairly,
transparently and only for defined educational, safety, pastoral and administrative
purposes.

## Working Commitments

- collect only information with an identified purpose
- provide a collection notice at or before collection
- protect information in transit and at rest
- restrict access according to role and need
- maintain correction, access, withdrawal and complaint pathways
- disclose information only for an authorised purpose
- maintain retention and secure-deletion rules
- record and respond to suspected privacy/security incidents
- assess service providers and cross-border handling
- avoid sensitive values in logs, URLs, analytics and ordinary email

The final policy must identify the governing legal entity, privacy officer, complaint
timeframes, applicable law, retention schedule, overseas disclosures and approved
exceptions.

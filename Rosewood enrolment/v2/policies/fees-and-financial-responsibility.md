The enrolment application records who should receive fee-account communication. It does
not itself create a fee debt or replace an approved fee schedule and enrolment agreement.

Families may nominate:

- joint responsibility
- one nominated account holder
- a proposed split arrangement requiring written school approval

Operational and legal responsibility cannot be inferred solely from a contact or
marketing preference. Court orders or private arrangements should be handled through a
restricted process.

The final document must specify amounts, billing dates, deposits, concessions, refunds,
withdrawal notice, debt processes, hardship support, split-account approval and dispute
resolution.

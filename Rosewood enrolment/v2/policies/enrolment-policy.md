## Purpose

Rosewood College intends to provide a clear, fair and family-centred process for
considering applications while planning for student wellbeing, learning and safety.

## Principles

- the dignity and best interests of each child
- partnership with parents as the primary educators of their children
- transparent criteria and communication
- reasonable adjustments and non-discriminatory consideration
- collection of only information reasonably required for enrolment and planning
- child safety, privacy and procedural fairness

## Application Stages

An expression of interest is not an application. An invitation to apply is not an offer.
An application is considered only after required information, evidence and signatures
are received. Interviews or planning conversations do not guarantee an offer. Enrolment
is final only after an authorised written offer and completed enrolment agreement.

## Considerations

Subject to final governance approval, considerations may include available year-level
places, the planned 2027 intake, capacity to provide a safe and suitable program,
sibling/family connection and alignment with the school's educational partnership.
No final priority order is established by this placeholder.

## Information And Adjustments

Families are invited to describe a student's strengths, needs and adjustments. This
information is used for planning and must not be treated as an automatic barrier to
consideration. Rosewood may seek relevant reports or a collaborative planning meeting
with consent.

## Decisions, Review And Withdrawal

Families will receive a written outcome and a contact point for questions. Final policy
must define review, waitlist, withdrawal, deferral, false-information and record-
correction procedures before use.

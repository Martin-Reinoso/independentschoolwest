# Child Safety And Wellbeing Commitment

> **DRAFT PLACEHOLDER - NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION**
>
> Version `draft-2026-08-02`.

Rosewood College is committed to creating a culture in which children and young people
are safe, heard, respected and supported. Child safety is everyone's responsibility.

Rosewood intends to:

- take all concerns and disclosures seriously
- value student voice and participation
- establish child-safe recruitment, conduct and supervision practices
- identify and reduce physical, online and organisational risks
- promote cultural safety and inclusion
- support students with disability and diverse needs
- maintain accessible complaints and reporting pathways
- respond promptly to suspected abuse, harm or grooming
- meet reporting, recordkeeping and information-sharing obligations
- review its child-safety systems with families and students

The final commitment and related policies must align with all applicable Victorian
Child Safe Standards and approved Rosewood governance arrangements.

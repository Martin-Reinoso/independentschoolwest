# Digital Technologies Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT PLACEHOLDER - NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION**
>
> Version `draft-2026-08-02`.

Digital technologies at Rosewood are intended to support learning, communication,
creativity and safe participation.

Users must protect credentials and personal information, use school systems for
authorised purposes, communicate respectfully, respect copyright and privacy, report
unsafe content or contact, and follow age-appropriate staff directions.

Users must not bypass security controls; impersonate others; access, create or share
harmful or unlawful material; bully or harass; disclose another person's information;
or install/use unapproved services on managed devices.

The final policy must define monitoring, filtering, personal devices, generative AI,
recording, incident response, consequences and separate age-banded student agreements.

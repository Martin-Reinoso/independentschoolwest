# Parent, Guardian And Carer Partnership Code

> **DRAFT PLACEHOLDER - NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION**
>
> Version `draft-2026-08-02`.

Rosewood's educational model recognises parents as the primary educators of their
children and seeks a respectful partnership between home and school.

Families and Rosewood personnel are expected to:

- place student safety, dignity and learning first
- communicate honestly, respectfully and through appropriate channels
- protect the privacy and reputation of students, families and staff
- raise concerns early and engage constructively in resolution
- support reasonable attendance, punctuality and learning expectations
- respect the Catholic identity and educational mission of the school
- avoid threatening, discriminatory, harassing or unsafe conduct
- follow child-safety directions and lawful school procedures
- keep important contact, health, care and authority information current

The final code must define complaints, unacceptable conduct, procedural fairness,
support, consequences and review rights.

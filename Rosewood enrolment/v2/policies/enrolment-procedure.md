## Family Journey

1. Rosewood records an expression of interest.
2. Authorised staff may issue a time-limited private invitation.
3. The invited guardian verifies the nominated mailbox with an OTP.
4. The family completes the application and uploads required evidence.
5. Each required guardian reviews the same frozen revision and signs independently.
6. Rosewood confirms receipt only after the required signature set is complete.
7. Authorised staff review completeness and may request clarification securely.
8. The family may be invited to a planning conversation/interview.
9. Rosewood issues a written outcome and, where applicable, a separate offer and
   enrolment agreement.

## Staff Controls

- verify invitation authority before issue
- never transmit identity, health, court or residency documents through ordinary email
- record material corrections and decision reasons
- separate incomplete draft visibility from submitted-application access
- use role-based access and named organisation accounts
- action correction, withdrawal and deletion requests through an approved workflow
- never override guardian/signatory requirements without a documented authorised reason

## Exceptions And Support

Final procedure must cover inaccessible email, expired invitations, disputed authority,
informal carers, separated parents, court orders, inability to upload, language support,
reasonable adjustments, duplicate records and technical failure.

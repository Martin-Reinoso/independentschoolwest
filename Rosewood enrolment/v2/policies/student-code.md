# Student Code Of Conduct

> **DRAFT PLACEHOLDER - NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION**
>
> Version `draft-2026-08-02`.

Rosewood intends to form a community in which students grow in faith, character,
knowledge, responsibility and care for others.

Students will be supported to:

- treat every person with dignity and respect
- participate purposefully in learning
- act honestly and accept responsibility
- care for property and the environment
- follow reasonable safety directions
- seek help when they or another person feel unsafe
- use technology responsibly
- resolve conflict without bullying, violence or humiliation
- contribute to the life, prayer and service of the school community

The final code must be age-appropriate and define support, restorative responses,
serious misconduct, procedural fairness and family communication.

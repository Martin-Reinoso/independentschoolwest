Thank you for sharing in this important moment for Rosewood College.

Dear families and friends,

Last week, families and supporters gathered online for our Family Information Evening. It was a joy to share the story of Families for Education, hear from our Founding Principal, Dr Anne-Marie Irwin, and mark the opening of Applications for Enrolment.

Whether you joined us live or are catching up now, thank you for the prayers, encouragement and practical support that continue to carry Rosewood College forward.

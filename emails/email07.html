A New Chapter

Applications for Enrolment are opening

On Wednesday 26 August, families and supporters are warmly invited to a special Rosewood College Information Session as we mark one of our most important milestones yet: the opening of Applications for Enrolment.

Our Founding Principal, Dr Anne-Marie Irwin, will join us as we look back at how Families for Education began and share the encouraging progress now bringing Rosewood closer to opening in 2027.

Progress to Share

Rosewood is moving forward

During the session we will share encouraging news from the registration journey, including our progress through the financial viability stage of the VRQA process and the generous support of Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools, which has made land available in Cobblebank for Rosewood's initial location.

We will also explain what the opening of Applications for Enrolment means for families and the next steps in becoming part of Rosewood College's inaugural community.

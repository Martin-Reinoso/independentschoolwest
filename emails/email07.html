A milestone years in the making

Applications for Enrolment are opening

This is a remarkable moment for Rosewood College and for every family who has helped bring the vision this far.

On Wednesday 26 August at 8:30 p.m., families and supporters will gather for our Family Information Evening as we announce the opening of Applications for Enrolment—a major step towards welcoming Rosewood's inaugural students in 2027.

We'll share encouraging progress on Rosewood's registration journey and exciting news: through the generous support of Melbourne Archdiocese Catholic Schools, a new site has been made available for the College's proposed initial campus.

Emotion Coaching with Katrina Alvir

Originally scheduled for Friday 28 August, our next Ideals in Teaching evening will now be held on Friday 11 September.

What if the hardest emotional moments could become opportunities for connection?

Every parent knows the moment: a toddler's meltdown, a child's frustration or a teenager's disappointment. Educator and positive psychology practitioner Katrina Alvir will introduce Emotion Coaching—a practical, encouraging approach that helps parents respond calmly, recognise what children are feeling, and guide them towards regulation, resilience and confidence. Come away with ideas you can use at home straight away.

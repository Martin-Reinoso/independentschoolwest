Getting to know our families

Anne-Marie’s September visit

During her recent visit to Melbourne, our Founding Principal, Dr Anne-Marie Irwin, met with families and their children for enrolment interviews. Thank you to everyone who made time to meet with her and share in these conversations about their children’s education.

These personal connections are at the heart of the partnership between home and school that we want to nurture at Rosewood. It is encouraging to see our community taking shape, one family at a time.

Looking towards 2027

Alongside the enrolment process, preparations for Rosewood’s proposed opening in 2027 remain focused on school registration and planning for our initial campus in Cobblebank. We look forward to sharing further updates as this work progresses.

Applications for Enrolment remain open. If your family is considering Rosewood, you can request a private application link through our website.

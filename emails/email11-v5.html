Celebrating our land blessing and the generosity bringing Rosewood closer.

A blessed beginning

On 17 October, families and supporters gathered with Fr Anthony Bernal for the blessing of Rosewood College’s first campus site in Cobblebank. Together, we prayed for the children, families and teachers who will give life to our College.

The celebration continued with a picnic at Bridge Road Recreation Reserve, giving families a chance to get to know one another while the children played.

Thank you to Fr Anthony, everyone who helped organise the day, and all the families who joined us. Seeing so many of you gathered together made the future of Rosewood feel that much closer.

Preparing for Rosewood’s future

We continue to work with the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority (VRQA) on school registration and with Melton City Council on the approvals needed for our initial campus. These processes are central to confirming when Rosewood can welcome its first students.

Please keep these approvals, our team and our future families in your prayers as we prepare to open our doors.

Applications for Enrolment remain open. We warmly welcome interested families to begin the application process and will keep you informed as the opening timeline becomes clearer.

Celebrating our land blessing and the generosity bringing Rosewood closer.

A blessed beginning

On 17 October, families and supporters gathered with Fr Anthony Bernal for the blessing of Rosewood College’s first campus site in Cobblebank. Together, we prayed for the children, families and teachers who will give life to our College.

The celebration continued with a picnic at Bridge Road Recreation Reserve, giving families time to meet, share a meal and enjoy the afternoon together.

Thank you to Fr Anthony, everyone who helped organise the day, and all who joined us. It was a joy to see our hopes for Rosewood reflected in the families gathered on the land.
